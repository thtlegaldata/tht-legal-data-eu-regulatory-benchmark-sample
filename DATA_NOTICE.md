# Data Notice

This repository contains a limited public technical sample of the THT Legal Data benchmark structure.

It does not contain the full benchmark, complete evaluation suites, standardized RAG test sets, production-ready input/output datasets, fully defined retrieval-context structures, or framework-specific benchmark formats.

The sample is provided for inspection and discussion of benchmark structure, multilingual alignment, source traceability and potential evaluation relevance.

The underlying legal texts are derived from official EU legal sources. The benchmark structure, selection, alignment organization, metadata and QA methodology are part of THT Legal Data.

No open dataset license is granted for the full benchmark. The public sample is provided for inspection and discussion only.

This repository should not be treated as a legal information product, legal AI application, compliance tool, legal advice resource, benchmark leaderboard, or production evaluation suite.
